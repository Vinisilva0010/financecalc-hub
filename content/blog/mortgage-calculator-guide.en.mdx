# Mortgage Calculator 2026: How to Accurately Calculate Your Monthly Payments

Before signing any mortgage contract, you need to clearly understand how much you will pay every month and how much will go toward interest over the years. In 2026, with still elevated interest rates, a small calculation mistake can cost you tens of thousands of dollars.

In this guide you will learn:

- How a mortgage payment is actually calculated
- The difference between principal and interest
- The safe limit for your housing payment
- How to use a precise calculator to simulate your numbers

## 1. What makes up a mortgage payment?

Every mortgage payment is composed of:

- **Principal (Amortization)**: the portion that actually reduces your loan balance
- **Interest**: the cost charged by the lender on the remaining balance
- **Taxes and Insurance** (in many countries): property taxes and home insurance

In the early years of the loan, most of your payment goes toward interest. Over time, more of the payment goes toward reducing the principal.

## 2. Why accurate calculation matters

Small differences in interest rate or loan term can change the total amount you pay by a very large margin. That’s why using a precise calculator is essential.

## 3. Safe income limit

A widely recommended rule is:

> Keep your total housing payment under **30%** of your gross monthly income.
